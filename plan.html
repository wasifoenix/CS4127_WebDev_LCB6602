Plan Your Visit

Everything you need to know before you travel

Getting There

Most international visitors arrive by air at Brunei International Airport, which is a short drive from the capital, Bandar Seri Begawan. Some visitors also arrive by road from Malaysia's Sarawak state.

Getting Around

  • Taxis: available at the airport and in the city. Ask the driver to use the meter.
  • Car hire: a good choice if you want to explore at your own pace. Drive on the left.
  • Water taxis: the quickest way to reach Kampong Ayer and the river sights.
  • Buses: a simple, low-cost way to travel between towns.
  • Dart: Cab Booking Application

Entry and Visa Guidance

Visa rules depend on your nationality and change from time to time. Always check the official information before you book your flight.

Read the latest entry requirements on the Brunei Government, or ask your nearest Brunei embassy or consulate. Make sure your passport is valid for the whole of your stay.

Where to Stay

Brunei offers hotels, guesthouses and homestays, mostly in and around the capital.

  • Hotels: the most common choice, with good facilities in the city centre.
  • Guesthouses: smaller, friendlier places that are often good value.
  • Homestays: a chance to live with a local family and learn about daily life.

Booking ahead is recommended, especially during public holidays and festivals.

Best Time to Visit

Brunei has a hot and humid tropical climate all year round, with temperatures usually around the high 20s to low 30s Celsius. Rain can fall at any time of year, so pack a light raincoat and an umbrella.

Many visitors choose to travel during the drier months, and they plan their trip around public holidays and festivals, which can be lively and fun to join.
